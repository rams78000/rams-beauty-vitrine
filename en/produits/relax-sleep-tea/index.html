A botanical moment

Relax & Sleep is our infusion concept inspired by verbena, chamomile, lavender and lemon balm. A botanical, floral universe for your pause.

Ingredients & flavour

Verbena · Chamomile · Dried lavender · Lemon balm. The recipe, proportions, plant origins and allergen information will be confirmed on the final label.

Preparing your ritual

Choose a cup, measure the infusion, pour hot water and steep before straining. Exact quantities, temperature and steeping time will be provided on the final packaging.

Formats & storage

Proposed formats: 50 g or 100 g loose leaf, 20 tea bags and a 100 g ritual gift set. Final gift contents remain to be confirmed. Store sealed in a dry place, away from heat and light.
